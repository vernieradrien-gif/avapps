Privacy policy

!ncognito – Impostor games · AVApps · Version française · Versión en español

In short

!ncognito does not collect any personal data. The app requires no account or sign-up, shows no ads, contains no analytics or tracking tools, and does not even have permission to access the Internet: nothing leaves your phone unless you decide so.

Who are we?

!ncognito – Impostor games is developed by AVApps, an independent developer. For any question: avapps.support@gmail.com.

Data stored on your phone

To work, the app only stores on your phone: the player names you enter, the scores and statistics, your settings (theme, language, durations, categories, selected words, mimes and missions) and the draw counter.

This information is never sent to AVApps or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time from the app (Settings, player list), by clearing the app's data in Android settings, or by uninstalling it.

If you have turned on Android backup to your Google account, Android may include this information in your phone's encrypted backup. This backup is managed by Google; AVApps has no access to it.

Permissions

The app only uses the vibration motor, to accompany the game (role reveals, start and end of mimes, end of the timer). It does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.

Idea or bug: when you write to us

The “Idea or bug?” button opens your mail app with a message ready to complete. Nothing is sent until you tap “Send” yourself. For a bug report, the message contains the app version, the Android version and the phone model; you can see it and edit it before sending.

We then receive your email address and the content of your message, through the Gmail service (Google). They are only used to reply to you and to improve the app. They are never sold, shared or used for advertising, and are deleted on request, or at the latest 3 years after our last exchange.

Donations

The “Support the app” button opens AVApps' Ko-fi page in your browser. Payment is handled entirely by Ko-fi and its payment providers (such as Stripe or PayPal), under their own privacy policies; AVApps never sees your bank details. Ko-fi only sends us the amount and, if you provide them, your name or nickname and your message.

Donations are optional and unlock nothing: the whole app stays available to everyone.

Children

The app does not collect any data, including children's data. The games are meant for a family audience; younger players should play with an adult.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you can ask to access, correct or delete the information you sent us by email, by writing to avapps.support@gmail.com. You can also file a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page and in the app, with its update date.
